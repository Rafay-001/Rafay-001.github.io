Loan Portfolio Delinquency & Credit Risk

Repayment behaviour across a lending portfolio, built to support risk monitoring and collections. It goes past simple delinquency counts into DPD bucketing, value-at-risk and a cure-vs-chronic split.

51.88%of loans currently delinquent
$106.8Mat risk, 18.94% of portfolio value
33.51%of loans in the 90+ DPD (NPL) bucket
47.4%of ever-delinquent loans eventually cure

The key finding: 99.3% of loans show a shortfall at the first checkpoint, so a first-checkpoint rule would flag almost the entire book. Predicting cure likelihood is the more useful target.

Method
  1. Validated data quality and excluded 28 loans (0.05%) with implausible planned-to-issued ratios.
  2. As-of matched cumulative planned vs. actual payments per loan, avoiding fan-out from ID-only joins.
  3. Bucketed by days past due: Current, 1–29, 30–59, 60–89, 90+.
  4. Took the portfolio snapshot at each loan's latest due checkpoint to avoid double counting.
  5. Classified loans as never delinquent, cured or chronic.
Recommendations
  • Build a cure-prediction model instead of flagging on first-checkpoint status.
  • Prioritise the 90+ DPD segment for active collections.
  • Investigate risk drift across June–August cohorts (49.97% → 53.99%).
  • Rank collections by dollar exposure, not loan count.
